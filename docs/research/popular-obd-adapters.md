# Popular Bluetooth OBD2 adapters (EU/US 2025-2026): what they speak and what K-Line Dash must handle

Research date: 2026-10-22. Builds on `elm327-clones.md` (clone behaviour, K-line) and `esp32-obd2-lessons.md` (real Konnwei KW905 + Corolla). Not repeated here: ELM response format, K-line init, `ATZ` timing, `?` tolerance.

Legend: **[V]** verified in a manufacturer page/manual, official docs, primary source code, or on our own hardware (KW905). **[R]** reported: forum, retailer, review, third-party code or a secondary doc (some of those are partly machine-written; treat as hints). **[U]** uncertain / sources conflict / not found.

Sourcing limits (be aware): there is no public per-model teardown database. Chip claims on retailer/review sites ("genuine ELM327 v2.2", "STN2120") often conflict; the only reliable way to know is to probe the unit (`ATI`, `AT@1`, `STI`, see section 5). Prices are what listings showed in 2025-2026 and move a lot. No sourced market-share numbers exist; "best-selling" is inferred from Amazon "bought in past month" badges and review counts.

Behaviour that matters:
- **MTU:** default ATT MTU 23 gives 20-byte writes and notifications. Many cheap units stay at 23 (KW905 worked entirely at 23 [V]). OBDLink CX supports up to 247 and warns that apps that fail beyond 20 bytes do not handle MTU [V]. Always reassemble notifications until `>`.
- **Write type:** CX FFF2 accepts both; KW905 FFF2 accepts only write-without-response [V]. Some clone firmwares reportedly drop bytes with write-without-response [R, miskibin doc]; our `BleTransport` already picks the type from properties.
- **Discovery:** KW905's full service discovery stalls after handle 25 on two stacks; filtered search works [V]. Dual-mode devices must be connected with `TRANSPORT_LE` (we do) or Android may use BR/EDR and return status 133 [R]. Status 133 is usually fixed by `close()` and retry after ~500 ms [R].
- **One central at a time:** a phone app still connected hides the adapter [V on KW905].
- **Advertised names:** Vgate BLE `IOS-Vlink`, `vLinker MC-IOS`; Veepeak `VEEPEAK`; Konnwei `KONNWEI`; OBDLink `OBDLink CX`; generic `OBDII`, `OBDBLE`. Many dongles advertise only a name, so scan unfiltered [R].
- **CCCD:** setting the local notification flag is not enough; the descriptor write must complete before the first command [R, and our code already does this].

## 3. Bluetooth Classic specifics

- **PINs:** 1234 (default), 0000, 6789, 7890, 1111 (Car Scanner's list) [V https://www.carscanner.info/en/android-bluetooth]. Konnwei 1234 [V]. Veepeak 1234 [V]. Vgate 1234 [V]. OBDLink LX/MX+: no PIN, secure pairing after pressing the **Connect** button [V official]. OBDLink CX (BLE): 123456 on old stacks, bonding only in the first 5 min [V]. Cheap units that stop accepting 1234 usually need a Bluetooth cache clear on the phone [R].
- **Adapters that hide until a button is pressed:** Konnwei KW905 [V], Vgate vLinker MC+ (black button) [V], OBDLink MX+/LX (Connect) [V], iCar2 BT3.0 (wake button) [R]. The UI should say "press the button on the adapter" after failures, not just retry.
- **Dual-mode adapters show two names:** Classic `...-Android`/`Android-Vlink`/`KONNWEI OBDII`/`VEEPEAK`, BLE `...-IOS`/`IOS-Vlink` [V Vgate pages]. Pair the Android (Classic) one; do not pair the iOS one.
- **Classic vs BLE choice on Android:** Car Scanner's author recommends Classic on Android when both exist, BLE is not faster [V]. Exception: our KW905 refuses Classic entirely [V]. A failed Classic attempt right before a BLE attempt disturbed BLE on KW905 [V].
- **Connect fallbacks already implemented:** secure SPP, insecure SPP, hidden `createRfcommSocket(1)`. Other apps additionally try channels 2-3 and the hidden `createInsecureRfcommSocket(int)` [R, B4X and CANblue threads in `elm327-clones.md`].
- **Android 12+:** needs `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` (runtime, "Nearby devices"); scanning `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` [V Car Scanner troubleshooting]. After pairing some phones show "Saved" instead of "Connected", which is harmless [V Veepeak]. Android head units: Veepeak says they "may not be supported"; check that the unit's pairing PIN is enabled and 1234 [V]. See also `headunit-autostart.md`.
- **Only one app can hold the RFCOMM channel;** a background app holding it blocks you [V Car Scanner].
- **Speed (old data, Android 4.4-6, treat as relative):** 3-channel CAN poll: OBDLink MX/LX ~6-8 Hz, BAFX 5-6 Hz, Vgate 3.9-4.8 Hz (and failed on one VPW GM), clone Wi-Fi 2.3 Hz [R]. https://forum.hptuners.com/showthread.php?57494-OBD-II-Device-Performance-

## 4. Protocols and "any car"

Coverage by era (no sourced market-share numbers exist; qualitative only):

| Era / market | Link layer | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| US 2008+ (mandatory) | ISO 15765-4 CAN 11-bit/500 kbit (protocol 6) | US 1996-2007: J1850 PWM (Ford), VPW (GM/Chrysler), some ISO 9141 | https://www.csselectronics.com/pages/obd2-explained-simple-intro [V]; 40 CFR 86.1806-05 |
| EU petrol 2001+, diesel 2003/2004+ (EOBD) | CAN **not** mandated in the EU; in practice ISO 9141-2 / KWP2000 (5-baud or fast init) until ~2004-2008, CAN afterwards | Our Toyota is ISO 9141-2; "any car from ~2008" is mostly CAN [U, no sourced EU split] | https://www.obd-ninja.com/en/help/features/obd-protocol [R] |
| CAN variants | 11-bit/500 (6), 29-bit/500 (7), 11-bit/250 (8), 29-bit/250 (9). 29-bit mostly trucks/some EU/Asian | ATSP0 finds them; the probe takes several seconds on clones | https://github.com/erdesigns-eu/Delphi-OBD/blob/main/docs/PROTOCOLS.md [R] |
| Mid-2000s to early-2010s Toyota/Lexus on CAN | Some do not answer the broadcast request ATSP0 sends, so "won't connect"; fix is `ATSP6` + physical addressing | https://www.obd-ninja.com/en/help/features/obd-protocol [R] |

Multi-PID on CAN:
- The J1979 allows up to **six PIDs in one Mode 01 request, CAN only**; the answer is one message, multi-frame if it exceeds 7 data bytes [V ELM327 datasheet: https://elmelectronics.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/ELM327DSL.pdf and the v1.4b datasheet quoted in `elm327-clones.md`].
- Real effect: Car Scanner's "optimize requests" claims up to 6x on CAN 11/29-bit [V https://www.carscanner.info/en/optimizing-connection-speed]. It also notes that reading from different ECUs forces the adapter to be reconfigured before each request, so keep to one ECU per cycle [V].
- **Clone behaviour (all [R]):** ARM-based clones truncate long requests to the first two bytes, so a batch silently degrades to one PID or fails ([miskibin doc](https://github.com/miskibin/obd2-dashboard/blob/main/docs/research-obd2-protocol.md)); a SwiftOBD2 user got `?` for concatenated PIDs ([discussion](https://github.com/kkonteh97/SwiftOBD2/discussions/35)). STN and vLinker (1024-byte requests) are fine [V manufacturer]. Genuine ELM327 v1.4b+ documents it [V]. PIC18F25K80 "v1.5" clones: [U], probe.
- **ECU side can also refuse:** some ECUs answer only the first PID or nothing to multi-PID requests [U, folklore, no source]. Hence probe + fallback.
- Design tip: a response with `41` + up to three 1-byte PIDs (or RPM + speed) fits one 7-byte CAN frame and needs no ISO-TP reassembly; 4-6 PIDs return `0:`/`1:` segment lines when `ATCAF1` is on, which our `ElmParser` may not yet handle (check before shipping).
- **Speed levers other apps use (all [V] unless noted):** `ATST` lower (hex x4 ms; default 0x32 = 200 ms); Car Scanner author: Viecar stable at 32, fastest at 16, cheap adapters unstable even at 64, old VW/Audi/Skoda need ~96; `ATAT1`/`ATAT2` adaptive timing; response-count digit (`010C1`, v1.3+); fixed protocol instead of ATSP0; `ATSH 7E0` physical addressing (plus `ATCRA 7E8` where supported, clones often answer `?`) [R]; headers off and spaces off to cut bytes; STN-only `STPX h:7E0, d:010C, r:1` collapses header+request into one round trip [R https://github.com/lazniak/OBDLink_MX-documentation/blob/main/pl/commands/st-commands.md].
- **ATSP0 order/timing:** auto search is optimised, not 1..9; allow 20-25 s on clones (`esp32-obd2-lessons.md` item 9). J1850 car: 10.4 kbit VPW is slow, expect 2-4 Hz [R HP Tuners].
- **BLE hurts polling rate:** default connection interval ~30-50 ms; `requestConnectionPriority(HIGH)` can roughly double the achievable rate [R miskibin doc, estimates, not measurements]. Our `BleTransport` does not request it yet (grep: no match).

## 5. Recommendations for K-Line Dash (prioritised)

1. **Make the BLE profile selection robust (`transport/BleUart.kt`).** Keep P1 first. Add, in this order: P2 (18F0, exists), **P3 E7810A71/BEF8D6C9 single characteristic** (generic fallback would catch it but explicit is faster and safer), **FFE0 with FFE1 notify + FFE2 write** (Deep OBD layout; today only the generic path covers it), P5 (exists), Microchip ISSC (cheap fallback). In `findBleUart`'s generic path: skip `1800/1801/180A/180F/FEF5` and TOPDON `0783B03E...`, prefer services whose UUID is `FFxx`/`18F0`, and **verify by probe**: after enabling notifications send `ATI\r` (or `ATZ`), and if nothing ELM-like arrives within ~2.5 s, try the next candidate pair (this is how the DongleScope tool finds read/write characteristics by injecting `ATE0`). Also resolve notify/write roles from properties only, ignoring list order. Update the "no serial BLE service" message to list what was found.
2. **Locked-adapter detection with a clear message** (new `AdapterCompat` in `app/transport` plus ConnectionScreen string). Three signals: (a) advertised-name patterns `BlueDriver`, `FIXD`, `TOPDON_`/`TopScan`, `ThinkDiag`/`Thinkcar`/`THINKTOOL`, `X431`/`EasyDiag`/`Golo`/`M-Diag`, `ANCEL`, `Autel`/`MaxiAP`, `FastLink`, `Carly`, `OBDeleven`; (b) GATT services that match none of the known UART profiles and whose probe (rec. 1) gets no reply; (c) two failed `ATZ` attempts with no bytes at all. Message: "This adapter only works with its own app and cannot be used by K-Line Dash. Compatible adapters: ..." with a link to the in-app list (rec. 10). Keep Carista and FIXD as "unverified, try it" rather than "locked". Never send commands beyond `ATZ`/`ATI` to a device flagged locked.
3. **Adapter fingerprint after `ATZ` (Elm327Session):** run `ATI`, `AT@1`, `STI` once (2 s each, `?` is a valid answer). `STI` answering `STN...`/`OBDLink...` = STN family (OBDLink LX/MX+/CX, and probably vLinker/iCar Pro 2S [U]); banner `v2.1` = ARM reimplementation (warn, expect `?`); `v2.2`/`v2.3` = Vgate/Veepeak+ class; `v1.5` = PIC18F25K80 clone class. Then capability flags, each a single probe whose `?` is stored: `supportsAt2`, `supportsCra` (bare `ATCRA` -> OK), `supportsPps` (read-only `ATPPS`), `supportsCountDigit`, `supportsMultiPid` (rec. 5). Persist per adapter address like the saved protocol so reconnects skip the probes. Surface the result in the Debug screen ("Adapter report", copyable). Do not use `ATTP A0`/`ATSP0` first on a unit flagged as the "hangs on ATSP0" class.
4. **Add per-adapter tweaks as data, not code:** `AdapterProfile(namePattern, preferredTransport, needsButton, pin, quirks)`. Seed with the rows in section 1a: `KONNWEI` -> BLE preferred for KW905 (Classic refuses [V]), `Android-Vlink`/`vLinker * -Android` -> Classic (Android), `IOS-Vlink` -> BLE, `VEEPEAK` -> Classic on Android per Car Scanner's advice, with BLE fallback; `OBDLink CX` -> BLE, request MTU 247; `OBDLink LX/MX+` -> Classic, "press Connect". Fallbacks: if preferred transport fails twice, offer the other one, but wait 1-2 s between a failed Classic attempt and a BLE attempt on a DUAL device.
5. **CAN multi-PID batching in `ObdPollScheduler` with detection and fallback.** Preconditions: protocol is CAN (`ATDPN` 6-9 or A6-A9), `supportsMultiPid` unknown or true. Probe once per adapter + protocol, after the supported-PID chain: send `01 <p1> <p2>` using two supported 1-byte PIDs, require both `41 p1` and `41 p2` blocks in the reply; then try 3 PIDs, then up to 6 (multi-frame). Store `maxBatch` (1 = off). Batch construction: only PIDs of the same ECU, ordered by tier; first fill **single-frame batches** (response `1 + sum(1 + len)` <= 7: RPM+speed, or three 1-byte PIDs); only use 4-6 PID batches if the parser handles `0:`/`1:` segment lines. Decode by PID id with lengths from `Signal`, never by position. Fallback triggers: `?`, `NO DATA` where the single PID worked a moment ago, a missing PID block twice in a row, or a `BUFFER FULL`/`STOPPED` -> `maxBatch=1` for this session and remember per adapter. Never batch on K-line/J1850 (CAN only, and the Toyota path stays one PID per request). Add the response-count digit only after `ATSH 7E0`-style physical addressing or a measured single responder. Expected win up to ~6x per Car Scanner, but unverified by us: log per-cycle latency (already planned in `esp32-obd2-lessons.md` #1).
6. **Classic connect tweaks (`BtClassicTransport.kt`).** Add as strategy 4/5: hidden `createInsecureRfcommSocket(1)` and `createRfcommSocket(2)`. Keep `cancelDiscovery()`, the 12 s timeout and the 300 ms retry delay. If all strategies fail and the device is not bonded, offer an in-app "Pair" action (`createBond()`) with PIN hints 1234 / 0000 / 6789 / 7890 / 1111. Add the "press the button on the adapter / ignition on" hint after the first failure for names matching the button list (Konnwei, vLinker MC, OBDLink). Treat "Saved but not connected" as normal, not as a failure to pair.
7. **BLE transport (`BleTransport.kt`):** call `requestConnectionPriority(CONNECTION_PRIORITY_HIGH)` right after services are ready (drop to BALANCED when the app is idle/ACC off); keep MTU optional but request 247 (CX allows it); keep the write type from properties, but if the first command times out on a no-response write while the characteristic also supports write-with-response, retry once with response. Add the KW905-style "filtered search" fallback already noted in `esp32-obd2-lessons.md` #7.
8. **Capability-tolerant init:** keep `SETUP_COMMANDS` best effort (`ATE0` is the only must-have), remember which optional commands returned `?` per adapter so reconnects do not repeat them, and never retry `ATZ` more than twice. Persist the fingerprint (rec. 3) so unsupported commands (ATPPS, ATCRA, ATAT1, ATR1 on v2.1 clones) are skipped, not re-sent.
9. **Adaptive timing as an opt-in "Faster polling" mode:** default `ATAT1`; on STN/vLinker/v2.2 classes try `ATAT2` and a lower `ATST` (start 0x20, step down toward 0x10) with automatic back-off after any `NO DATA`/`DATA ERROR` burst. Old VW/Audi/Skoda needs ~0x60 [V Car Scanner]. Expose the chosen value in the Debug screen.
10. **Compatibility list in the app and README.** Three tiers:
    - *Recommended:* OBDLink LX (Android), MX+, CX (BLE), Vgate vLinker MC+/BM+/FS/MS, Vgate iCar Pro 2S, Veepeak OBDCheck BLE+ (Classic or BLE) and VP11 (PIC18F25K80).
    - *Works, with caveats:* Konnwei KW902 (Classic), KW905 (BLE only, press the button), generic PIC18F25K80 "v1.5" Classic units, Viecar, BAFX, Panlong, Vgate iCar Pro BLE (profile varies).
    - *Not supported:* "v2.1" ARM clones that answer `?` to the essentials, BlueDriver, FIXD (unverified), ThinkDiag/ThinkCar, Launch Golo/EasyDiag, TopScan, ANCEL BD200, Autel MaxiAP, any dongle that only works in a vendor app.
    Add a "Test my adapter" button that runs rec. 3/5 and prints a copyable report; publish crowd-sourced results in `docs/adapters.md` so the list grows from real data.
11. **Debug export of the GATT table:** services, characteristics, properties, negotiated MTU and advertised name as text (copy/share). This is the single cheapest way to collect new profiles from users with unknown adapters (same data an nRF Connect dump gives) and feeds rec. 1.
12. **Scan and device list:** extend `OBD_NAME_HINTS` in `BluetoothDevices.kt` with `IOS-VLINK`, `ANDROID-VLINK`, `VLINKER`, `VEEPEAK`, `LELINK`, `KIWI`, `TONWON`, `OBDBLE`, `V-CHECKER`, `OBDII`, `OBD2`, `ELM327`, `KONNWEI`, `MAXIOBD`, `HGC`; add a separate "known locked" list to show a warning badge instead of hiding the device. Keep scanning unfiltered. Show Classic and BLE entries of one dual-mode box grouped with a hint about which one to pick.
13. **Power/sleep UX:** expect auto-sleep ~30 min on Vgate/Konnwei/Veepeak+, ~3 mA on vLinker/iCar Pro 2S/OBDLink [V]. On repeated connect failures show "adapter asleep? Replug it or press its button; turn the ignition on." Mention that cheap clones without sleep drain the battery (already in `elm327-clones.md` section 6); advise the user once.
14. **Wi-Fi presets:** `192.168.0.10:35000` (default), PLX Kiwi Wi-Fi `192.168.0.10:30000`, OBDLink MX Wi-Fi `192.168.0.74:23` [R https://github.com/evrenonur/obd2-elm327-pid-reference/blob/master/OBD2_Complete_PID_Reference_EN.md]; SSIDs `WiFi_OBDII`, `V-LINK`, `OBDII`.
15. **Simulator and tests:** add scripted adapters to `SimulatorTransport`: (a) v2.1 clone answering `?` to ATAT1/ATPPS/ATCRA, (b) one that truncates multi-PID requests (answers only the first PID), (c) STN answering `STI`, (d) 20-byte split notifications with `>` alone in a chunk, (e) a silent "locked" device. These exercise recs. 2, 3 and 5 without hardware.
